Accommodation

Choose accommodation that matches your budget and comfort. The options below describe common types of stays; they are examples, not confirmed listings or live availability.

Mountain lodge

A comfortable place to relax after a day of hiking, often with indoor facilities and mountain views.

  • Comfort-focused
  • Good for couples and families
  • Check meals and transport

Guest house

A welcoming stay that may offer a chance to learn about local life and culture.

  • Friendly atmosphere
  • Ask about breakfast
  • Confirm location before booking

Camping

An outdoor option for travellers who want a closer connection with nature.

  • Bring suitable equipment
  • Check toilets and water access
  • Follow campsite rules

Before paying, confirm the accommodation's real name, location, prices, safety, and availability directly with the provider.
