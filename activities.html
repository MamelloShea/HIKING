Activities

Hiking tours can include different outdoor and cultural experiences. Select activities based on your fitness, interests, and the weather.

🥾

Guided hiking

Walk with a knowledgeable guide, follow marked or agreed routes, and learn about the local landscape.

Bring: sturdy shoes, water, snacks, and warm layers.

📷

Nature photography

Capture mountain peaks, valleys, wildlife from a safe distance, and changing light across the landscape.

Remember: respect people and ask before taking their photographs.

🏘️

Cultural visits

Learn about Basotho traditions and community life through respectful visits arranged with local hosts.

Remember: follow local customs and ask permission before entering private areas.

🌄

Scenic viewpoints

Pause at safe viewpoints to enjoy the landscape and take a break during your route.

Remember: keep away from unstable edges and steep drop-offs.
