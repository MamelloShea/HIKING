Explore the highlands

Tourist Destinations

Lesotho is known for its high mountains, open valleys, and traditional villages. These sample destinations can inspire a hiking trip. Confirm local access, guides, and conditions before travelling.

Maletsunyane area

Enjoy mountain scenery and walking routes around the Semonkong area. Ask a local guide about suitable trails and current conditions.

Suggested for: scenic walks and photography.

Thaba Bosiu

Explore a place with important Basotho history and views of the surrounding landscape. Respect heritage sites and follow visitor guidance.

Suggested for: history and gentle hiking.

Lesotho Highlands

Discover rugged peaks, quiet valleys, and highland paths. Some routes can be demanding and require preparation.

Suggested for: experienced hikers and nature lovers.

Safety tip: weather can change quickly in the mountains. Check the forecast and hike with suitable equipment and a knowledgeable guide.
